ACME TECHNOLOGIES - FINANCIAL EXPENSE POLICY
Fictional sample document for software testing only.
Effective date: 1 October 2026

1. MEAL REIMBURSEMENT
Business meals are reimbursed up to INR 1,200 per employee per day.

2. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per night.

3. CLAIM DEADLINE
Employees must submit expense claims within 15 calendar days.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
Claims require an itemized receipt, purchase date, amount, business purpose,
and project or department. Claims above the limit require manager approval.

5. EXCLUSIONS
Personal purchases and traffic fines are not reimbursable.

6. PROCESSING TIME
Finance reviews complete claims within 7 business days.

International travel allowance is not specified in this sample policy.
This fictional document is not a real company policy or financial advice.
