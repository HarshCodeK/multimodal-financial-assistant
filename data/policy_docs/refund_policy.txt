REFUND POLICY

Refunds are issued within 30 days of the original purchase date. To be eligible
for a refund, the customer must not have used the service beyond a single test
call during the trial period. Requests made after the 30 day window are not
eligible for a monetary refund, but may be converted into service credit at the
discretion of the support team.

Partial refunds are granted when only some line items are disputed. The refund
amount is the sum of the disputed items only; taxes are refunded in proportion
to the refunded items. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, and
may take a further 5 to 10 business days to appear on a statement.

Subscription renewals are refundable pro rata when a cancellation is requested
before the next billing date. Usage charges incurred during the current period
are not refundable, because the capacity has already been consumed.
