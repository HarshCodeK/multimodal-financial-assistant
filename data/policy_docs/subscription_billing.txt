SUBSCRIPTION BILLING

All subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis. An annual
plan is invoiced once per year at the committed rate. Changing plan mid-cycle
takes effect at the next renewal, and the difference is not prorated.

Overage charges are assessed after the included allowance is exhausted, at the
per-unit rate published on the pricing page. Overage is billed in arrears with
the following month's invoice rather than immediately.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Any request
received during a billing cycle does not receive a refund for the unused
remainder of that cycle; it simply stops further charges.
