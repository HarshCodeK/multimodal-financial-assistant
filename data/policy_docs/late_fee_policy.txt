LATE FEE POLICY

A late fee of 1.5 percent of the outstanding balance applies to any payment not
received by the due date shown on the invoice. The fee accrues daily at 1.5
percent of the original outstanding amount, not of the running balance, and is
capped at 15 percent of the invoice total.

A grace period of 5 calendar days applies to invoices under 50 in total. No late
fee is assessed during the grace period. Accounts more than 30 days overdue are
suspended automatically, and reactivation after suspension requires payment of
all outstanding amounts including accrued fees.

Disputed invoices are not subject to late fees while the dispute is open. To
open a dispute, contact billing support with the invoice number and the line
items in question within 60 days of the invoice date.
